Selected work

01

XO robot arm

Assistive design · Concept sketching · Mechanism design · CAD design tables · Laser cutting

Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD) is a genetic disorder that progressively weakens the skeletal muscles. Recent studies found that, even though the muscles no longer react, electromyographic (EMG) signals can still be measured. The XO robot arm is therefore programmable to do simple tasks.

After brainstorming the project’s technological challenges, two ideas were selected to be investigated thoroughly. One of the three concepts was then modelled and adapted for laser cutting using design tables, and built as a prototype.

02

Farma

Service + product concept · Modular design · Ideation · Form studies

Farma connects farmers with customers through a distribution service for (ugly) crops. Instead of visiting every farm separately, customers use one central selling point: a modular dispenser in their neighbourhood. We want to be seen as the distribution service, not the brand selling the food, and we speak to eco-friendly, environmentally interested people.

For farmers

Farmers sell their crops to us and do no extra work: once a week a driver picks up the stock. We rent spare storage space from farmers, which is already equipped to store crops. That keeps costs low, so we can buy at a higher price and sell for less. Farmers can also rent out space, where we store crops from other farms when needed.

For customers

One central selling point instead of many farm visits. Crops are ordered ahead, picked up from the dispenser and sold by weight, with a tolerance the customer can set. Quality is checked before the crops go into the dispenser.

The dispenser

Designed to be modular, so each neighbourhood gets what it needs. Components attach to one another to increase the capability of a single dispenser. The sketches explore stackable modules with a glass top and a cabinet with a lid and a drawer.

03

Komtai oven toaster

Product concept · 3D modelling · Rendering

A toaster oven that keeps the classic look people like, with convection cooking and a smarter way of managing its own heat. The boards explain the exploded parts, the airflow around the food, how the shell stays cool, and the touch display with two dials. They are written in English and Persian.

04

WeGo

Product + app concept · Storyboarding · Rendering

A physical stack of layers that goes with the WeGo app. The storyboard follows two friends: one presses their stack, it pops open, the other’s pops open too, and they press back.

SolidWorks studies

SolidWorks · Assemblies · Renders

Three models that show the range: a hand tool kit with its small parts, a garden planter, and a large playground assembly.

About

Hi, I’m Maede. Studying Industrial Design Engineering at the University of Twente taught me the whole route from brainstorming and ideation to sketching, concept design and prototyping. The subjects were not always the ones I would have picked, but the method stuck.

Since then most of my published work has been digital: web development, branding and product design. Next to that I kept my hands in the physical side. I model and 3D print in SolidWorks, and I worked part-time in quality control, where I read technical drawings every day.

I have been thinking about moving back to the technical side of my studies for a year. A junior industrial designer role is exactly that.
